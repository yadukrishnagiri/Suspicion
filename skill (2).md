# Imposter — Skill Guide

## Citizen Tips
- Be indirect
- Avoid definitions
- Avoid obvious clues
- Listen for hesitation
- Look for context mismatches

## Imposter Tips
- Listen before speaking
- Follow themes, not exact words
- Talk about situations and experiences
- Stay adaptable

## Strong Clues
Contextual:
weekend
celebration
routine
travel

## Weak Clues
Definitions.
Synonyms.
Direct descriptions.

## Golden Rule
Help your side without revealing the answer.
