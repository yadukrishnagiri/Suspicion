# Suspicion

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Users and purpose

A group of 3–15 people sharing one phone for an in-person social deduction game. Talking and voting happen in the room. No account is required. The browser is also a supported way to play.

## Capabilities and constraints

Welcome, player entry, mode/category selection, ordered private reveal, discussion, elimination, and results remain in their existing order. Three modes and eight categories are provided. The discussion starter stays fixed. Elimination exposes a role only. Citizens win when all imposters are eliminated; imposters win at parity. Rematch keeps settings; Change game keeps names; New group returns to entry. Names are suggested from device-only history.

## Brand commitments

Backstage Masquerade replaces the detective identity: ink plum, ivory, vermilion, brass, expressive bundled display type, readable sans type, mask artwork, theatrical welcome and verdict motion. No generic card grids, gradients, glass or decorative emoji. Touch and hold the card itself to reveal; release immediately hides it.

## Accessibility

48 dp controls, readable contrast, system font scaling, keyboard operation, reduced motion, safe areas and concealed secrets when focus is lost.

## Implementation agreement

Existing Expo SDK 57 and Zustand rules stay. Figma file creation succeeded but canvas access hit the Starter MCP quota. On October 7, 2026 the user approved direct app implementation and a local design handoff instead of waiting for Figma.
