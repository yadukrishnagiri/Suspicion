# Imposter — Official Rules

## Objective
Citizens must eliminate all imposters.
Imposters must survive until active imposters >= active citizens.

## Modes
1. Main Word vs Imposter Word
2. Main Word vs Imposter Hint
3. Blind Imposter

## Setup
- Max players: 15
- Max imposters: 7
- Minimum players = (2 × imposters) + 1
- One category per game

## Discussion
The app does not control discussion or voting.

Players decide:
- speaking order
- voting
- tie resolution
- whether to eliminate anyone

## Elimination
Tap a player.
Reveal only:
- Citizen
- Imposter

Never reveal words.

## Win Conditions
Players Win:
All imposters eliminated.

Imposters Win:
Active imposters >= active citizens.

## New Game
New:
- words
- imposters
- discussion starter

Existing participant names remain saved.
